ANK YUDH is an exciting number puzzle game where strategy, speed, and smart moves come together. Combine matching numbers, build your highest tile, and challenge yourself to reach 4096!

🎮 Features:

🔢 Classic number-combining gameplay
🏆 Milestones from 64 to 4096
⏱️ Time-based challenges
🥇 Best Score & Best Time
⚔️ Yudh Record
🔊 Sound & pause controls
📱 Smooth gameplay on mobile and PC
💾 Progress and records saved locally

Can you reach 4096 and become the Yudh Champion? ⚔️🔥
